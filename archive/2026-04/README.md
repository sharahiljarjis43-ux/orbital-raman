# Earlier Raman materials — April 2026

These files preserve the original public repository contents before the October 24, 2026 update. They are retained for traceability and comparison with the current source.

The figures, CSVs, interactive demonstrations and manuscript draft reflect earlier model versions. Their numerical values and wording may rely on assumptions that have since been qualified. In particular, modeled detection, infrared proxy scores, chemical class labels and wavelength enhancement must not be read as independently measured performance or chemical abundance.

- `README.original.md` preserves the earlier overview, including its historical claims and file layout.
- `src_fixed.py` preserves the earlier model source. The current model is at the repository root.
- `simulator.html` and `orbital_raman_simulator.jsx` are earlier interactive demonstrations; they are outside the current reproduction checks.
- PNGs and CSVs are historical output snapshots.
- `paper_final (1).docx` is a historical manuscript draft and is not presented as a peer-reviewed publication or a current submission-ready report.

The two previously published mission products were moved to `Data/` with their contents preserved. No new laboratory measurements were added.

Use the [current Raman overview](../../README.md) and [methods and limitations](../../docs/METHODS_AND_LIMITATIONS.md) for the current interpretation, or the [research portfolio](../../RESEARCH_PORTFOLIO.md) for the other projects.
